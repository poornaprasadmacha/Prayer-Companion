Prayer Companion — User Guide & Manual

Welcome to Prayer Companion! This app is your peaceful, quiet sanctuary for daily prayer and communion with God. Here is everything you need to know explained in simple and easy steps:

1. Daily Praying & Focused Prayed Dropdown

  • To Pray Today: On the Home screen, you see all active prayer points waiting for your prayer today.
  • Tap "Pray": When you finish praying for a point, tap the Pray button. It turns into a sight-friendly olive green Prayed ✓ badge.
  • Toggled Down by Default: Once marked as prayed, the card immediately moves down into the collapsed "Prayed Today" section. It stays toggled off (closed) by default so you won't accidentally pray for it twice and can keep 100% of your concentration on the remaining prayer points.
  • Reviewing Prayed Points: Want to see what you prayed today? Simply tap ▶ Prayed Today anytime to expand the list.

2. Adding & Managing Prayer Points

  • Add New Point: Click + New Prayer Point (or Quick Add) to add a prayer with a title and optional details.
  • Clean Layout: Cards show the Title first and Description below without clutter or distracting badges.
  • Card Menu (···): Click the 3 dots on any card to Edit, Archive, Delete, or ask community companions to pray for you.

3. Prayer Companions (Community Tab)

  • Instant & Seamless: Community prayer points are saved in your device's local database (IndexedDB). When you open the Companions tab, points show up instantly with zero loading delay!
  • "I'll Pray" Commitment: Click I'll Pray to let a fellow believer know you are standing with them in prayer.
  • Encourage: Send a short scripture or word of comfort.
  • Ask for Prayer: Choose Ask Others to Pray for You on your prayer card. You can share with your name or 100% anonymously.

4. God's Answers & Thanksgiving Journal

  • Mark as Answered: When God answers a prayer, tap the menu and choose Mark as Answered. Write down your testimony to remember God's faithfulness!
  • Thanksgiving Tab: Use the Thanks tab to write down daily blessings and reasons to give thanks to the Lord.

5. Eye Comfort (Olive Green) & Dark Mode

  • Sight-Friendly Olive Green: The app uses an eye-comforting olive green color palette designed specifically to protect your vision and reduce eye fatigue.
  • Dark Mode Toggle: Tap the Moon/Sun icon in the top header anytime to switch between peaceful light and dark viewing modes.

6. Offline-First & Daily Email Reminders

  • Works 100% Offline: Everything is stored safely on your phone/computer. When online, changes sync automatically to your Google Sheets database.
  • Email Reminders: In Settings, choose your preferred morning and evening times to receive gentle reminder emails with your active prayer points.
