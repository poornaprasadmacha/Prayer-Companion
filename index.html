Prayerly — User Guide

1. Daily Devotion & Scripture

Curated Bible verses, reflection quotes, and active prayer priorities at a glance.

2. Pray & Focus

Tap "I Prayed" to complete a point; it moves down so you stay focused on remaining needs.

3. Prayer Companions

Intercede for fellow believers anonymously, send encouragement notes, and commit in prayer.

4. Testimonies & Gratitude

Record answered prayers with testimonies and keep a daily gratitude praise journal.

5. 100% Offline & Private

Runs locally on your device with IndexedDB. Automatically syncs with the Cloud when connected.

6. Automated Reminders

Set custom morning and evening times to receive gentle reminder emails with active prayer points.
